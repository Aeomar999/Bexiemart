Addresses fail contrast
Pickup and drop-off render in text-muted-foreground (#94A3B8) on white — 2.6:1, under the 4.5:1 minimum. They are the most important words on the screen and the hardest to read in sunlight. They go to #0F172A at 15/600.
Payout leads
"Food Delivery" and "GH₵ 12.00" were both 16–18px bold, so accept-or-skip took two reads. The fee goes to 28px, the type becomes its label, and distance and time — currently absent — sit opposite it.
One card, not a card in a card
The sheet held a bordered grey card holding the route and the slider. Dropping that inner surface returns 32px of padding and removes a border that meant nothing.
Status pill earns its space
A 64px bar for a toggle and two words becomes 52px carrying today's earnings and trip count — the numbers riders currently open the Earnings tab for.
Re-centre moves from below the header to just above the sheet — thumb reach, and clear of the status card. Slider 64→56 with a solid brand fill; the 12%-opacity track read as disabled.

Same fix as the vendor screen
This file is a near copy of the vendor earnings screen, so it takes the same treatment: the balance takes the money gradient and level-2 depth with the figure at 44px, Overview folds into the hero as a hairline strip, transaction rows go 72→56px, five entries show instead of two.
Trips joins the strip

Transaction titles become the destination rather than the generic "Delivery payout" — the rider recognises the trip, not the payout type. Currency prefix drops from every row.

Metrics at 14px
Trips and revenue — the two numbers a rider checks — are 14px bold beside a 14px icon, smaller than every row label on the screen. The card they sit in is 66px of mostly padding.
Metrics join identity
A hairline strip inside the identity card carries trips, revenue and rating at 20/800. One card instead of three, and the whole screen — including log out — now fits without scrolling.
Vehicle rows read as values
Label above value in a 72px row made "Type / Motorbike" look like a heading and a caption. Label left, value right at 12px muted matches every other settings row in the app.
Vehicle and plate also appear in the identity meta line, so the Vehicle group becomes confirmation rather than the only place to find them. shadow-lg removed on both groups.
