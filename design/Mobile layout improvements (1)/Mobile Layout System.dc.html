Spacing, rhythm and hierarchy

One spec for customer, vendor and rider screens. Tokens and gutters stay as they are in global.css. What changes is which values a screen may use, how many, and — new in this revision — where depth is allowed.

Revised after the wallet review
The wallet screens set the direction: gradient money surfaces, real depth, and a number as the loudest thing on the page. §8 of DESIGN-SYSTEM.md ("flat, zero shadow") is superseded by §04 below — depth is now permitted, but only on money and only at the levels defined here.

A page title (display-sm 700) and a section head (heading-md 700) are two steps apart on a screen full of bold text, so they read as one level. Section heads become uppercase labels, and — following the wallet — the number is the biggest thing on the page.

05

Quick actions

Two languages exist today: 56px saturated circles on the wallet, 48px tinted tiles on home and the vendor dashboard. Split by job. Actions — top up, send, add item, withdraw — take the wallet's filled circle: a stronger tap target that survives a busy background. Navigation — the home Explore grid, category tiles — keeps the boxed tinted tile, because a grid of eight destinations reads better as a set of surfaces than as a row of buttons. What is retired is the near-white #f8fafc tile on a white card, which has no edge at all.

07

Rules that fix the named problems

Floating bar covers content
The quick-actions dock moves into the scroll flow directly under the search bar. Bottom padding drops from 160px to 96px, so the last row of content is reachable.
Sections feel like a random list
Every section is label → content at a 24px gap, and rails alternate with grids rather than stacking four rails in a row. The label is the rhythm.
Too many sections on home
Nine blocks become five. Filter pills fold into the nav grid; Top Products, New Items and Most Popular become one rail with segments. No content is dropped.
Inconsistent card padding
Two values, chosen by role not by screen: 16 for anything holding data, 20 for the one money hero. Card padding="lg" is retired.
No breathing room
Room comes from fewer nested surfaces, not bigger gaps. Order cards lose their inner grey item box; the rider sheet loses its card-in-a-card. Gaps stay tight at 16/24.
Depth is reserved, not banned
Shadows now carry meaning: if it lifts off the page it is money or it is chrome. The stray shadow-lg on settings groups, profile cards and order cards still comes off.
Off-palette blues
topup.tsx hardcodes #1d4ed8 and #3b82f6 for the selected payment method. Both become brand-700, so the one selected state matches every other selected state.
Weak type hierarchy
18px/700 section headlines become 12px uppercase labels, and the money scale gives balances a 44 / 32 / 20 ladder. The loudest thing on a screen is now always a number.
