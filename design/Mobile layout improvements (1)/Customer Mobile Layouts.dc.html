The dock covers the tab bar
At bottom: 24 the 120px dock sits on top of the 114px tab bar, and the scroll view pads 160px to compensate. Its five actions duplicate four of the six Featured Highlights.
One nav grid, in flow
Dock + highlights + filter pills merge into eight 74px tiles. Nothing floats, nothing is duplicated, and the whole nav set is 176px instead of 335px.
Nine sections become five
Top Products, New Items and Most Popular were three rails in a row; they become one rail with segments. Flash Sale folds into the Deals tile.

What the rest of the app copies
The 44px balance with the currency on the baseline, the gradient plus hue-matched shadow, and the 52px saturated quick-action circles. All three now appear on the vendor and rider balances, the cart total bar, the order-tracking hero and the home promo banner.
Recent activity never clears the fold
Stack 260 + actions 80 + rewards 130 with 32px gaps between fills the whole 686px content area. The transaction list — the only part of the screen that changes day to day — starts 1px below it.
Stack 260 → 196, balance unchanged
Layer offsets tighten to 0 / 10 / 60 and the peeking card shows name, last four and brand only. The 44px figure keeps its size — it is the one number in the app that has earned it.
Rewards becomes a row
Tier, coins and the action stack to 130px. Side by side they fit in 76px and gain a real "Redeem" button instead of a "How to earn" pill that looks like a badge.
Two things to check in build
The masked number at white/80 drops under 4.5:1 on the lighter hashed gradients — amber gold and slate especially; it goes to full white. And the layer top values currently branch on card count (0/14/82, 0/68, 42) — one offset scale should cover 0, 1 and 2+ by collapsing layers.
